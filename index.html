Wheel Throwing

From my AP 3-D Art and Design portfolio Linda Alles Memorial Award · The Winsor School's top junior art award

A sustained investigation into the tension between lines and curves. I carved horizontal ridges into round stoneware vessels and changed their depth, spacing and number from piece to piece. The outsides stay bare so light and shadow show the form. Only the interiors are glazed.
